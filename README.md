## About Stypendless

**Stypendless** is a virtual internship platform designed to make practical industry experience more accessible to students, freshers, and aspiring professionals.

We believe professional experience should begin before graduation.

Through structured, self-paced virtual internships, participants work on practical assignments inspired by real-world industry requirements, develop hands-on skills, and demonstrate their work through GitHub repositories.

No complicated application processes. No internship participation fees. Just structured learning, meaningful work, and an opportunity to build experience.

## How It Works

<p align="center">
  <strong>REGISTER → RECEIVE → BUILD → SUBMIT → GET REVIEWED → COMPLETE</strong>
</p>

**01 — Register**

Choose an internship domain and duration that aligns with your interests.

**02 — Receive Your Offer Letter**

Get your internship details and access to your dedicated workspace.

**03 — Work on Practical Tasks**

Complete structured weekly assignments and a major project at your own pace.

**04 — Submit Through GitHub**

Share your project repository links for evaluation.

**05 — Get Your Work Reviewed**

Your submissions are reviewed, and feedback or revision requests are provided where applicable.

**06 — Complete Your Internship**

Successfully complete the required tasks and reach your internship completion milestone.

## Built Around Flexibility

Traditional schedules do not always fit students' academic commitments.

Stypendless follows a self-paced approach that allows participants to manage their internship alongside college, examinations, projects, and other responsibilities.

- Start whenever you are ready.
- Complete weekly tasks ahead of their suggested schedule.
- Track your progress through a dedicated workspace.
- Participate in multiple internship domains.

The focus is on completing meaningful work, not simply counting attendance.

## Our Internship Philosophy

We believe an internship should be more than an offer letter or a certificate.

It should involve:

- Practical problem-solving.
- Consistent effort.
- Technical exploration.
- Independent execution.
- Demonstrable project work.
- Constructive evaluation.

Our objective is to encourage participants to build skills they can demonstrate, explain, and confidently present.

## Optional Completion Certificates

Internship participation, task submission, and evaluation are free of charge.

Participants who successfully complete the required internship tasks may optionally request a completion certificate for a nominal fee.

Certificate issuance is subject to successful evaluation and the applicable verification process.

Certificates are not mandatory for participating in or completing an internship.
