How Stypendless Operates

A four-step practical framework designed for autonomous execution, hands-on learning, and verifiable code.

STEP 01

Free Registration

Select your domain and duration. Your internship account is instantly provisioned with a generated Intern ID and official offer letter record.

STEP 02

Weekly Tasks

Access intermediate, industry-relevant milestone tasks in your workspace. Work at your own pace without rigid joining batches.

STEP 03

GitHub Submissions

Push code to your personal GitHub repository and submit the repository URL for review. No mock portals or simulated quizzes.

STEP 04

Optional Certificate

Upon successful approval of all milestones, celebrate your accomplishment. An optional, verified completion certificate is available upon request.

Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version: v1.0-2026

1. Internship Framework & Eligibility

Stypendless offers self-paced, virtual, task-oriented internships designed for college students, diploma candidates, fresh graduates, and aspiring professionals. Participation in the internship, access to weekly tasks, submission evaluation, and offer letter generation are completely free of charge.

2. Task Submissions & Code Integrity

All weekly milestone tasks and capstone project deliverables must be submitted solely via public GitHub repository URLs. Submissions must represent original work. Plagiarism, deceptive submissions, or automated repository spoofing will result in immediate termination of the internship workspace without certificate eligibility.

3. Self-Paced Schedule & Milestones

Interns may complete structured tasks ahead of the nominal weekly schedule. While nominal deadlines are provided for structure, autonomous self-paced completion is fully supported within the total duration selected.

4. Optional Paid Completion Certificates

Participation and task evaluation are free. Official completion certificates with public QR-code verification are completely optional and paid. Pricing is tiered by duration (1 week: ₹149+, 2 weeks: ₹249+, 3 weeks: ₹349+, 1 month: ₹499+, 2 months: ₹999+). An administrative early-completion surcharge of ₹50–₹100 may apply if a certificate is requested prior to the scheduled completion date. Purchase is strictly voluntary.

5. No Guarantee of Employment

Stypendless provides practical skill development and proof-of-work validation. Stypendless does not promise, advertise, or guarantee job placements, employment contracts, monetary stipends, or recruitment partnerships.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version: v1.0-2026

1. Data Collection

We collect essential student details required for account authentication and internship record-keeping: Full Name, Email Address, Indian Mobile Number, LinkedIn Profile URL, Selected Domain, Duration, and optional academic information (College, Year, Semester).

2. Purpose & Use of Information

Your data is used strictly for: (a) Authenticating your login sessions; (b) Creating and verifying your internship enrollment and unique Intern ID; (c) Generating your official offer letter and optional completion certificate; (d) Delivering essential system notifications.

3. Data Security & Storage

Credentials and records are secured using enterprise-grade Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore. We never store raw passwords on our servers. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies.

4. Student Rights & Account Management

You have the right to review, update, or request deletion of your account records by contacting our administrative team at support@stypendless.com.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Version: v1.0-2026

1. Free Platform Policy

All core internship operations—including application, registration, task access, code review, and mentor feedback—are 100% free of charge. Therefore, no refunds apply to internship participation itself.

2. Optional Certificate Purchases

Optional completion certificates are subject to clear terms:

  • Before Certificate Generation: If an optional certificate request was placed by mistake and the certificate record has not yet been processed or issued, a full refund may be requested within 48 hours of payment.
  • After Certificate Issuance: Once an official certificate ID, QR verification record, and digital credential have been generated and issued to the student, the purchase is final and non-refundable.

3. Early-Completion Surcharges

Any early-completion administrative surcharge (₹50–₹100) applied when requesting credentials prior to the scheduled duration completion date is non-refundable once the expedited administrative review has begun.

4. Refund Contact

To request a refund on an eligible optional certificate transaction, email support@stypendless.com with your transaction reference and Intern ID.
